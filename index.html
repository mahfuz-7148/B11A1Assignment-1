Pillars of "Bangladesh 2.0"

Innovation

Prioritize science, technology, engineering, and mathematics at all levels of education

Sustainability

Invest in solar, wind, and hydropower to reduce dependency on fossil fuels and ensure energy security

Infrastructure

Expanding broadband and 5G coverage to urban and rural areas to enable seamless digital communication
and e-commerce

Empowerment

Promote education for girls and marginalized groups to close gender and socio-economic gaps

The Vision of Future

Bangladesh 2.0 aims to redefine the nation’s trajectory by embracing cutting-edge technology, promoting inclusive growth, and ensuring sustainability. With initiatives in
renewable energy, digital transformation, and robust infrastructure, the country is set to become a global model of development.

Digital Transformation

Creating a nationwide digital ecosystem to
enhance connectivity and access to services for all citizens.

Green Energy

Investments in solar and wind energy to ensure
a sustainable and eco-friendly future.

Modern Infrastructure

Building state-of-the-art infrastructure to support
economic growth and global competitiveness.

Focus on empowering people through social business and microcredit,
enabling them to lift themselves out of poverty and become self-reliant
